## Chelsea neighbourhoods we cover

### King's Road
Mansion blocks, flats over shops and restaurants, and narrow side streets with resident parking. King's Road itself has loading bays, but you often need a bay suspension for the side streets. Blocks on the main road have porters and service lifts; flats above commercial premises usually have steep back staircases.

### Chelsea Harbour
A gated riverside development with strict move-in rules. [CONFIRM: Chelsea Harbour typically requires 48 hours' notice to book the service lift and a copy of our insurance certificate.] Parking is in the basement or designated bays, which means longer carries. We check the building's protocols on the survey and book the lift in advance.

### Cheyne Walk and Chelsea Embankment
Period houses facing the river, most of them four to six storeys with narrow hallways and no front parking. The Embankment is a controlled zone, and the nearest loading space is often 50 metres or more from the front door. Wardrobes and sofas need careful angling on the stairs, so we bring dismantling tools and protective sheeting for the banisters.

### Sloane Square and Sloane Street
Luxury flats in portered buildings, many with marble lobbies and strict move-in times. You'll need to book a slot with the concierge, and some blocks ask for the insurance certificate a week before the move. Service lifts are available, but you share them with other residents, so timing matters.

### World's End Estate
A Brutalist estate at the western end of King's Road, with tower blocks and low-rise flats. The estate office manages loading bays and lift bookings. [CONFIRM: World's End Estate loading bay rules and estate office contact details.] The lifts are large enough for most furniture, but access from the parking bays to the block entrances varies.

### Fulham Road corridor
Victorian and Edwardian terraces, split into flats, with some purpose-built blocks closer to South Kensington. Most streets have controlled parking and need a bay suspension. Staircases in converted houses are narrower than in mansion blocks, so large items often go through windows or need dismantling.

### Chelsea Village and Imperial Wharf
Modern apartment blocks near the river, most with concierge services and dedicated move-in procedures. You book the service lift and the loading bay together, and some blocks charge a refundable deposit for any damage. We note all the rules on the survey and handle the booking.

## Parking and access in Chelsea

Chelsea sits inside the Royal Borough of Kensington and Chelsea's controlled parking zones. [CONFIRM: RBKC currently asks for bay suspension applications at least 10 working days before the move; check the latest process and fees with RBKC highways.] We can arrange the suspension as part of the move, so book your date early.

Portered buildings need advance notice for move-in slots, and most ask for a copy of our insurance certificate before they confirm the booking. Service lifts are shared with other residents, which is why we agree a two-hour or four-hour window with the concierge rather than an all-day slot.

Streets like Cheyne Walk and parts of the Embankment have no front parking, which means longer carries. On the survey we measure the distance and plan the crew size accordingly.
