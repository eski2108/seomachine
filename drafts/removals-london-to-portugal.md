## Popular destinations in Portugal

**Lisbon**  
The capital draws expats to neighbourhoods like Alfama, with its cobbled lanes and tiled houses, Belém on the riverfront, and modern Parque das Nações. Alfama's narrow streets can be tricky for larger vehicles, so we check access on the survey and plan the crew and vehicle size accordingly.

**Porto**  
Portugal's second city sits on steep hills above the Douro. Ribeira, the old waterfront district, has tight lanes and limited parking. Foz do Douro, to the west, is flatter and easier for access. We note any access challenges and bring the right equipment.

**Algarve**  
The southern coast is popular with UK retirees. Faro is the regional capital; Lagos, Albufeira, Vilamoura and Tavira are resort towns with a mix of apartments and villas. Most properties have good road access, though gated communities may need advance notice for deliveries.

**Cascais and Sintra**  
Coastal Cascais and hilltop Sintra, west of Lisbon, attract families and retirees. Sintra's winding roads and historic buildings sometimes mean longer carries, which we plan for during the survey.

**Madeira and the Azores**

## What's involved in moving to Portugal from the UK

### Residence permit
UK nationals moving to Portugal permanently need a residence permit (título de residência). Apply via SEF (Serviço de Estrangeiros e Fronteiras) or AIMA within three to four months of arriving. You'll need proof of income or savings, health insurance and a Portuguese tax number (NIF).

### Customs and duties
Since Brexit, household goods moving from the UK to Portugal require a customs declaration. Personal effects and used furniture are duty-free if you've lived outside Portugal for at least 12 months and owned the items for at least six months. You'll need an inventory listing everything in the shipment and proof of your previous residence. [CONFIRM: does K1 handle customs paperwork, or does the client or a Portuguese agent complete it?]

### Pets
Your dog, cat or ferret can move to Portugal, but the pet passport scheme no longer applies to UK pets. Instead, you need an Animal Health Certificate (AHC) from a UK vet, issued no more than 10 days before you travel. Your pet must be microchipped and vaccinated against rabies at least 21 days before departure. Dogs also need tapeworm treatment one to five days before entry.

### Driving licence
Your UK driving licence is valid in Portugal for six months after you're granted residence. Exchange it for a Portuguese licence within that window and you won't need to retake the test.

### Tax number and bank account
You'll need a NIF (Número de Identificação Fiscal) to open a bank account, sign a rental contract or buy property. Apply at a Finanças office or through a representative.

### Healthcare
The UK Global Health Insurance Card (GHIC) covers temporary stays. Once you're resident, register with the Portuguese health system using your residence card.
