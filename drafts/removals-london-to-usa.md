## How your move to the USA works

### 1. Free survey
We visit your home (or do a video survey if you prefer) to see what you're moving, check access at both ends and answer your questions. After the survey, you get a tailored fixed quote in writing.

### 2. Packing day
On the day we've agreed, our crew arrives with export cartons, wrapping and wooden crates. Fragile items are wrapped individually; furniture is protected with blankets and corner guards; everything is labelled room by room for US customs and for unpacking at the other end.

### 3. Customs documentation
Before your shipment leaves the UK, we prepare an inventory list (item by item, room by room) for US Customs and Border Protection. You'll need your passport, visa or green card, and you may need to sign a power of attorney so our shipping agent can clear the goods through US customs on your behalf if you're not present at the port.

### 4. Sea freight to the USA
Your packed goods are loaded into a shipping container and shipped across the Atlantic. Transit time is typically four to six weeks to the East Coast (New York, Boston, Miami) and six to eight weeks to the West Coast (Los Angeles, San Francisco, Seattle).

### 5. US customs clearance
When the container arrives at the US port, it goes through customs. Personal and household goods entering the USA are usually duty-free as long as you've owned and used them for at least a year. Prohibited and restricted items (certain foods, plants, some electronics) will be flagged, so we go through the list with you before packing day.

### 6. Delivery to your new home
Once cleared, your shipment is delivered to your address. If your new property is on a narrow road or in a rural area where a large truck can't reach, we deliver to the nearest accessible point and arrange a smaller vehicle or long carry from there.

## US customs and paperwork for household moves

US Customs and Border Protection (CBP) allows you to bring your personal and household goods into the USA duty-free if you've owned and used them for at least 12 months before the move. New or commercial items may attract duty.

**Documents you'll need:**
- Passport and visa, green card or work permit
- Detailed inventory list (every item, room by room)
- CBP Form 3299 (Declaration for Free Entry of Unaccompanied Articles) if you arrive in the USA before or after your shipment
- Power of attorney for the shipping agent if you're not at the US port when the container is cleared

**Prohibited and restricted items** include certain foods, fresh plants, seeds, soil, some medicines, firearms and ammunition. CBP publishes the full list; we'll go through it with you on the survey so nothing gets packed that can't be imported.

**Electrical appliances:** the UK runs on 230V/50Hz; the USA runs on 120V/60Hz. UK appliances need step-down transformers to work in America, or you can replace them when you arrive. Most people find it easier to buy new once they're settled.

## Packing and shipping to the USA

### Export packing
Sea freight means your belongings spend weeks at sea, get loaded and unloaded from containers and may sit in a warehouse before final delivery. Export packing protects them through all of it.

We use heavy-duty cartons, bubble wrap, blankets and custom wooden crates for fragile or valuable items. Furniture is wrapped, legs are removed where needed and everything is secured so it doesn't shift in transit. Mirrors, glass and artwork get extra protection.

[Find out more about our packing services](/removals/packing-services/), or pack some items yourself and leave the rest to us (part packing).

### Transit times
- **East Coast (New York, Boston, Miami):** 4–6 weeks
- **West Coast (Los Angeles, San Francisco, Seattle):** 6–8 weeks
- **Inland and rural areas:** add 1–2 weeks for final delivery after the container clears customs

Every move is different, so we tailor every quote. Factors that affect the price include:

## FAQs

### How long does it take to move from London to the USA?
Sea freight takes four to eight weeks depending on your destination. East Coast cities like New York and Boston are faster (four to six weeks); West Coast and inland destinations take six to eight weeks or longer. [CONFIRM: Air freight takes 5–10 days but is more expensive.]

### Do I need to be in the USA when my shipment arrives?
Not necessarily. You can sign a power of attorney so our shipping agent clears the container through US customs on your behalf. If you're not at your new address when the goods are delivered, you can nominate someone else to receive them or arrange delivery for a later date.

### What can't I take to the USA?
US Customs restricts or bans certain foods, fresh plants, seeds, soil, some medicines, firearms, ammunition and items made from endangered species. We go through the prohibited list with you on the survey so nothing gets packed that can't be imported. [Read the full list on the CBP website](https://www.cbp.gov/).

### Do I pay US import duty on my household goods?
Personal and household goods you've owned and used for at least 12 months are usually duty-free. New items, commercial goods or large quantities of the same thing may attract duty. Declare everything accurately on the customs forms; penalties for false declarations are severe.
