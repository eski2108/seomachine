## Customs and quarantine rules for Australia

The Australian Border Force enforces strict biosecurity laws. Items commonly prohibited or restricted include:

- Untreated wooden furniture or items containing soil
- Fresh food, seeds and plant material
- Certain animal products
- Firearms and weapons (require permits)

Your belongings will be inspected on arrival. If you're moving on a permanent residency visa and your goods have been owned and used for at least 12 months, you can usually bring them in without paying GST [CONFIRM: current exemption rules]. Temporary residents may face different rules.

During the survey, we'll go through what you're planning to take and flag anything that might not clear customs. For the latest rules, check the [CONFIRM: Australian Border Force website or ask us to verify specific items].

## Moving to Australia from the UK: what to know

Most people moving to Australia from the UK hold a skilled migration visa, family visa or employer-sponsored visa. The type of visa you have can affect your customs treatment. Permanent residents usually qualify for duty-free import of personal effects; temporary residents may not. Check your visa conditions with the Australian Department of Home Affairs.

If you're also moving a car, boat or other vehicle, those need separate shipping and compliance paperwork. We can advise you during the survey or recommend a specialist if needed [CONFIRM: K1 does not ship vehicles].
