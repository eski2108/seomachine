## Popular destinations in Spain

### Madrid
Spain's capital sits in the centre of the country. Older buildings in neighbourhoods like Malasaña and Lavapiés often have narrow staircases and no lifts, so we check access on the survey and plan crew size and equipment accordingly. Parking in the city centre can be tight; your delivery crew will contact the building manager or community in advance if a bay suspension is needed.

### Barcelona
The Gothic Quarter, Gràcia and Eixample are full of beautiful but tricky apartment blocks: tall buildings, small lifts, winding staircases. We've handled moves into all of them. If your new home is in a pedestrianised street, we arrange the closest drop-off point and carry from there.

### Costa del Sol (Málaga, Marbella, Estepona)
Coastal moves come with their own considerations. Villa driveways can be steep or narrow, underground car parks have height restrictions, and gated communities may ask for advance notice and a delivery slot. Tell us the details on the survey, and we'll plan the delivery to fit.

### Costa Blanca (Alicante, Torrevieja, Benidorm)
Apartment blocks along the coast range from new builds with service lifts to older low-rise blocks with outside staircases. Long carries from the vehicle to the front door are common, especially in complexes set back from the road. We check this on the survey and factor it into crew size and time.

## Moving to Spain from the UK: what you need to know

Removals are only part of the picture. If you're moving to Spain permanently, you'll also need to think about residency, healthcare, schools (if you have children) and pet relocation if you're taking a dog or cat.

**Residency and NIE**: Since Brexit, UK citizens need a visa for stays longer than 90 days in any 180-day period. If you're moving permanently, you'll apply for a residence permit, which starts with getting an NIE (Número de Identidad de Extranjero), Spain's foreigner ID number. You'll need it to open a bank account, sign a rental contract and register with social security.

**Healthcare**: UK state pension recipients can use Spain's public healthcare system if they register and get an S1 form from the UK. If you're working in Spain, you'll pay into the Spanish social security system. If you're retiring early or self-employed, you may need private health insurance until you qualify for public care.

**Schools**: If you have school-age children, decide whether you want a state school (free, taught in Spanish or the regional language), a private Spanish school, or an international school (taught in English, follows the British or IB curriculum, and charges fees). International schools fill up early, so apply as soon as you know your moving date.

**Pets**: Dogs and cats need a microchip, an up-to-date rabies vaccination (at least 21 days before travel), and a vet-issued health certificate valid for 10 days. The certificate must be endorsed by the UK Animal and Plant Health Agency (APHA). If you're flying your pet separately, you'll also need an airline-approved crate and a booking with the cargo team.
