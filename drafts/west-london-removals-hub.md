# Removals in West London

K1 Removals is a professional removal company based in West London. Our office is at Gable House, 18–24 Turnham Green Terrace, Chiswick, London W4 1QP, and we've been moving homes and businesses across the capital for over ten years. We cover the whole of West London, from Hounslow to Hammersmith & Fulham, Richmond to Ealing, and from local moves within W4 to long-distance UK and international removals.

Every move starts with a free survey, in person or by video, so we can give you a tailored fixed quote in writing. We're insured up to £1 million, and we arrange parking suspensions as part of the move. Call **0330 103 0041** or [get a free quote](/contact/) online.

## Areas we cover in West London

We handle removals across every part of West London. Our crews know the streets, the parking zones and the building access rules, so your move is planned properly from the start.

### London Borough of Hounslow

**[Chiswick](/areas-covered/chiswick-removals/)** is our home. We know every street in W4, from the Victorian terraces in Bedford Park to the riverside flats along Strand on the Green and the mansion blocks around Turnham Green. Chiswick has narrow one-way streets, controlled parking zones and conservation areas, so we plan crew size, vehicle access and parking suspensions on the survey.

**[Brentford](/areas-covered/brentford-removals/)** sits between the A4 and the Thames, with a mix of canal-side flats, older terraces and new builds along the Great West Road. Access can be tight around the High Street and Boston Manor, so we check it before the move.

**[Isleworth](/areas-covered/isleworth-removals/)** has large Victorian and Edwardian houses near Syon Park and smaller terraces around Worton Road. Parking is easier than in central Chiswick, but the one-way systems need local knowledge.

**[Gunnersbury](/areas-covered/gunnersbury-removals/)** covers the postcodes between Chiswick and Acton. It's mostly Edwardian houses and 1930s semis, with stairs, tight hallways and bay suspensions needed on Pope's Lane and Gunnersbury Avenue.

### London Borough of Ealing

**[Acton](/areas-covered/acton-removals/)** runs from the Victorian streets around Acton Green to the larger houses in East Acton and the flats along the Uxbridge Road. We've moved families out of the conservation areas and businesses out of the studios near North Acton.

**[Ealing](/areas-covered/removals-ealing/)** covers W5 and W13, from the mansion blocks around Ealing Broadway to the semis in Northfields and the terraces in South Ealing. Parking suspensions need at least five working days' notice in Ealing.

### London Borough of Hammersmith & Fulham

**[Hammersmith](/areas-covered/hammersmith-removals/)** is a mix of mansion blocks, terraces and modern flats near the Broadway and along King Street. Lifts in the blocks help, but the traffic and parking around the Hammersmith flyover need planning.

**[Fulham](/areas-covered/fulham-removals/)** has narrow streets, controlled parking and mansion blocks with no lifts. We've moved hundreds of flats in SW6, and we know which streets need a suspension and which have loading bays you can book.

**[Shepherds Bush](/areas-covered/shepherds-bush-removals/)** and **[White City](/areas-covered/white-city-removals/)** cover the postcodes around the Westfield centre and the BBC. We move flats out of the managed blocks on Wood Lane and houses around Shepherds Bush Green.

### Royal Borough of Kensington and Chelsea

**[Kensington](/areas-covered/kensington-removals/)** has some of the tallest mansion blocks in West London. Lifts are small, service lifts need booking and some buildings ask for insurance certificates and evening move slots, so we arrange it all on the survey.

### London Borough of Richmond upon Thames

**[Richmond](/areas-covered/richmond-removals/)** covers TW9 and TW10, from the riverside houses near Richmond Bridge to the flats on Kew Road. It's in Richmond upon Thames, which asks for five working days' notice for parking suspensions.

**[Barnes](/areas-covered/barnes-removals/)** is a conservation area with narrow streets, residents' parking and strict building rules. Most moves need a suspension, and the larger houses need careful planning for furniture on the stairs.

**[Putney](/areas-covered/putney-removals/)** sits between the Thames and Wandsworth, with mansion blocks along the Upper Richmond Road and terraces around Putney Heath. We cover SW15 and the border with Wandsworth.

### London Borough of Wandsworth

**[Wandsworth](/areas-covered/wandsworth-removals/)** covers SW18 and the areas around Wandsworth Town and Southfields. It's technically South West London, but we move homes and offices there every week.

## Parking suspensions across West London

Most streets in West London sit inside a controlled parking zone. If you're moving on a weekday, you'll usually need a bay suspension so the lorry can park outside your door. The notice period depends on the borough:

- **Hounslow** (Chiswick, Brentford, Isleworth): 10 working days
- **Ealing** (Acton, Ealing): 5 working days
- **Hammersmith & Fulham** (Hammersmith, Fulham, Shepherds Bush, White City): varies by zone; some need 10 days, others 5
- **Richmond upon Thames** (Richmond, Barnes): 5 working days
- **Kensington & Chelsea** (Kensington): varies by zone

Double yellow lines need a separate dispensation, and some conservation areas have extra rules. We arrange the suspensions as part of the move, so book your date early and let us handle the paperwork. [Read our full parking suspension guide](/blog/parking-suspension-moving-day-london/).

## FAQs

**Do you cover all of West London?**  
Yes. We're based in Chiswick and we cover the whole of West London, including Hounslow, Ealing, Hammersmith & Fulham, Richmond upon Thames, Kensington & Chelsea and Wandsworth.

**Which boroughs do you work in?**  
We work across London, but our heartland is West London: Hounslow, Ealing, Hammersmith & Fulham, Richmond upon Thames, Kensington & Chelsea and Wandsworth. We also handle long-distance UK moves and international removals.

**Do I need a parking suspension for my move?**  
On most West London streets inside a controlled parking zone, yes. Hounslow asks for 10 working days' notice, Ealing and Richmond ask for 5, and Hammersmith & Fulham varies by zone. We arrange the suspensions as part of the move.

**How far in advance should I book a West London removal?**  
At least two to three weeks for a straightforward house or flat move, longer if you need a parking suspension or you're moving in the summer or at the end of the month. For office moves or international moves, book as early as you can.

**Can you move my office out of hours?**
