## Popular destinations in France

### Paris and Île-de-France
Apartment buildings in central Paris often have narrow stairwells, small lifts and strict rules about move times. Some buildings ask for advance notice and proof of insurance. We check access at the survey and plan the crew size, vehicle and any longer carries before the day.

### Provence and Dordogne
British expats often move to rural Provence or the Dordogne for the climate and slower pace. Village houses can have narrow lanes, limited parking and steep garden paths. We note all of this on the survey, so the crew arrives prepared.

### French Riviera (Nice, Cannes, Antibes)
Apartment buildings along the Côte d'Azur are similar to Paris: tight access, lift restrictions and concierge rules. Parking in Nice and Cannes is challenging, so advance planning is essential.

### Brittany and Normandy
Closer to the UK, Brittany and Normandy are popular with retirees and second-home buyers. Coastal towns and rural properties often have long carries from the road to the front door. We bring the right equipment and enough crew to handle it.

## Customs and paperwork after Brexit

Since the UK left the EU, every move from London to France requires customs clearance. You'll need:

- **Customs declaration**: a CN23 form listing what you're moving
- **Inventory list**: item-by-item descriptions and values
- **Proof of ownership**: receipts or bank statements for high-value items (antiques, jewellery, electronics)
- **Residence permit or visa**: UK citizens moving to France for more than 90 days need a long-stay visa or residence permit. This is your responsibility, not part of the removals service, but you should apply well before the move.

## Packing for an international move

International moves need stronger packing than UK house moves. Furniture travels wrapped in protective blankets, fragile items go in double-walled boxes with extra padding, and mattresses and upholstery are covered to keep them clean.

If you're packing yourself, use sturdy boxes, wrap plates and glasses individually, and mark fragile boxes on all sides. We supply packing materials or can handle the whole job for you. [Find out about packing services](/removals/packing-services/).

## What you can't take to France

Some items are prohibited or restricted on UK–France moves:

- **Plants and soil**: not allowed without a phytosanitary certificate
- **Food**: fresh meat, dairy and certain other foods are restricted
- **Alcohol and tobacco**: limited to personal allowances (check the latest duty-free limits)
- **Firearms and ammunition**: require import permits
- **Pets**: dogs and cats need a pet passport or health certificate and must travel under the PETS scheme, which is separate from the removals service

We'll confirm what can and can't be moved at the survey.
