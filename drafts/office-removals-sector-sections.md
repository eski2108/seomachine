## Sectors we move

K1 Removals is an established commercial removals company. Every business move is planned around how you work, so your team can get back to it fast. Every move is insured up to £1 million.

### School relocations in London

Moving a school means classrooms, offices, libraries and storerooms, usually inside a tight holiday window. We plan the move around your term dates, label everything by room, and set up furniture where you need it in the new building, so staff can walk in and start teaching. Whether it's one department or a whole site, we'll go through the plan with your business manager before moving day.

### Retail relocations

We move all types of retail, from single shops to larger stores: shop fittings, stock, back-office kit and fixtures. We work around your trading hours, including evenings and weekends where needed, so you lose as little selling time as possible. Stock is packed and labelled so it goes straight back onto the right shelves.

### Clinic relocations

Clinics can't afford long closures. We plan your move room by room, protect equipment and furniture in transit, and keep patient records and confidential files secure from start to finish. We'll agree a schedule that keeps downtime to a minimum, so you can reopen on time.

### Laboratory relocations

Lab moves need careful planning. We'll walk the site with your team, agree what moves when, and pack and handle equipment with care. Your staff stay in control of what's prepared before we move it, and we keep everything labelled so the new lab can be set up quickly.

### Business and document storage

If you're downsizing, refurbishing or moving in stages, we can store furniture, equipment, stock and archive boxes between moves. [See our storage services](/removals/storage-services/). Got paperwork you don't need any more? Ask about our [confidential document shredding](/removals/office/document-shredding/).
