## House moves by size

### 1 and 2-bed properties

Most first-time buyers and young families in London start in a 1 or 2-bed flat or maisonette. These moves are straightforward in terms of volume, but the access can be tricky: lifts, communal staircases, move-in slots and parking permits all need planning. See our [flat removals page](/removals/flats/) for the detail on apartment blocks and managed buildings.

### 3-bed family homes

The typical London family house move. A 3-bed property usually fills one large removal vehicle, and the move takes half to a full day depending on the floors, the parking and how much you've packed in advance. We bring the right-sized crew, dismantle beds and wardrobes if needed, protect your floors and doors, and reassemble everything at the new place.

### 4-bed house removals

Larger Victorian and Edwardian terraces and semis across West and South West London. A 4-bed house move needs careful access planning: steep staircases, narrow hallways, heavy furniture that won't turn the corners without dismantling. We note it all on the survey and bring the tools and crew to handle it. Packing can be full, part or customer-packed, depending on what suits you.

### 5-bed and large house removals

Georgian townhouses, detached properties and large family homes. These moves often need two vehicles or multiple trips, a bigger crew and more time. Full packing is common, especially if you're coordinating a chain and can't pack weeks in advance. We plan the vehicle size, crew and timing on the survey, so the move runs smoothly even when it's a big one.

## Parking and access in London

Most London streets inside controlled parking zones need a bay suspension for a removal vehicle. The rules vary by council: Hounslow asks for at least 10 working days' notice, Ealing and Hammersmith & Fulham have their own application processes, and Richmond's rules differ again. We arrange parking suspensions as part of the move, so you don't have to chase permits yourself. See our [parking suspension guide](/blog/parking-suspension-moving-day-london/) for the detail on each West London borough.

Victorian and Edwardian properties bring their own access challenges: tight hallways, steep staircases with turns halfway up, narrow front doors and low ceilings on upper floors. Large wardrobes, sofas and dining tables often need dismantling or careful angling to get them in and out without damage. We see it on the survey and plan accordingly.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does it cost to move a house in London?**  
Every house move is different. The cost depends on the volume of belongings, the access on both properties, the distance, whether you need packing and the date you're moving. We give you a fixed written quote after the free survey, so there are no surprises. [Get a free quote](/contact/) to see what your move will cost.

**Do I need a parking suspension for my house move?**  
If you're moving in or out of a controlled parking zone, yes. Most London boroughs require a bay suspension application at least 5 to 10 working days before the move. We arrange parking suspensions as part of the service, so you don't have to deal with council applications. See our [parking suspension guide](/blog/parking-suspension-moving-day-london/) for the detail on each borough.

**How long does a house move take in London?**  
A typical 3-bed house move takes half to a full day, depending on the number of floors, the parking distance on both ends and how much you've packed in advance. Larger 4 and 5-bed properties can take a full day or longer. We give you a realistic time estimate after the survey.

**What's included in the house removal service?**  
The survey, the fixed written quote, the crew, the vehicle, insurance up to £1 million, floor and door protection, furniture dismantling and reassembly, and an insurance certificate for your records. Packing is optional and quoted separately if you need it.

**Can you move a 4-bed or 5-bed house?**  
Yes. We move houses of any size across London and the UK. The survey ensures we bring the right-sized crew, the right vehicles and enough time to handle larger properties properly.

**What if my completion date changes?**  
Contact us as soon as you know. We'll try to move your booking if we can, and if you need to move out before your new property is ready, we can [store your belongings](/removals/storage-services/) short or long-term until you're ready to complete.
