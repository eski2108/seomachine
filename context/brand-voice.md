## Voice pillars
### 1. Established and professional
- **Sounds like**: a removals firm that has done thousands of moves; calm, confident, specific.
- **Example**: "We've been moving homes and businesses around West London for over ten years."
- **Avoid**: "man and van", "cheap", "budget", hype, exclamation marks.

### 2. Local and specific
- **Sounds like**: we know the street, the parking zone, the council rules and the building manager.
- **Example**: "Chiswick is in Hounslow, which asks for bay suspensions at least 10 working days before the move."
- **Avoid**: generic copy that could be any town; invented landmarks or rules. Verify every local fact.

### 3. Reassuring and careful
- **Sounds like**: we plan access, protect floors and doors, and are insured up to £1 million.
- **Example**: "We check access on the free survey and plan crew, vehicle size and any longer carry before the day."
- **Avoid**: promises we can't prove (no fake review counts, no "#1 in London").

### 4. Plain and direct
- Short sentences, UK English, second person ("you"), active voice.

## Tone by content type
- **Area pages**: local expert, street-by-street, parking/access detail, NAP for Chiswick.
- **Service hubs (house, office, international)**: authoritative, process-led (survey → fixed written quote → move day → aftercare).
- **Blog/guides**: helpful, practical checklists; soft CTA to a free survey.
- **Commercial**: business-like, minimise downtime, evening/weekend moves, insurance certificate for building managers.
