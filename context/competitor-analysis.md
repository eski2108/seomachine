## Patterns
- Local queries are won by thin, exact-match location pages. K1 can beat them with real local detail: streets, council parking rules, building access.
- Council parking-suspension pages rank for "removals [area]", so every area page needs a parking section.
- Office: specialists lead with trust signals (e.g. "DBS-checked crews"). Our edge: Chiswick base, insured up to £1 million, out-of-hours moves.

## K1 advantages
- The only real W4 office among Chiswick competitors (Gable House, Turnham Green Terrace): put NAP up front.
- Domestic, commercial and international under one brand.
