# K1 Removals Style Guide

## Hard rules (apply to every file in context/)
- K1 Removals is an established, professional removals company (London, whole UK, international). NEVER write "man and van" (not in titles, copy, alt text or anchors).
- Services: domestic (house/flat) removals, international removals, commercial/office removals, plus confidential document shredding and customer callbacks. Packing, storage and piano pages exist on the live site as part of a removal; do not invent any other service (no office clearance, IT asset disposal, crating, courier, car shipping, fine art) unless Dean confirms it.
- Insurance wording: "insured up to £1 million".
- No prices, ever. Use "Get a free quote" CTAs.
- Nothing is published without Dean's OK.

- UK English (organise, colour, metres), UK date format (24 October 2026).
- Phone: 0330 103 0041. Postcodes in capitals with a space (W4 1QP).
- Headings: sentence case. H1 pattern for area pages: "Removals in [Area], [Postcode]". Title pattern: "Removals [Area] [Postcode] | Local Removal Company | K1 Removals".
- Write "insured up to £1 million" (not "£1m cover" in body copy).
- Numbers: words for one to nine, digits for 10+.
- Short paragraphs (2–4 sentences); bullet lists for streets, steps, checklists.
- Every service/area page has an FAQ block (4–6 Qs) suitable for FAQ schema.
- No fake stats, review counts or awards. No AggregateRating schema on our own pages.
- CTAs: "Get a free quote" (primary). Brand colours for any design notes: navy #12285A, green #8CC63F, blue #3F7CC4.
- Sign internal notes and messages to the owner as Dean.
