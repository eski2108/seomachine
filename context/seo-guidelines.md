# K1 Removals SEO Guidelines

## Hard rules (apply to every file in context/)
- K1 Removals is an established, professional removals company (London, whole UK, international). NEVER write "man and van" (not in titles, copy, alt text or anchors).
- Services: domestic (house/flat) removals, international removals, commercial/office removals, plus confidential document shredding and customer callbacks. Packing, storage and piano pages exist on the live site as part of a removal; do not invent any other service (no office clearance, IT asset disposal, crating, courier, car shipping, fine art) unless Dean confirms it.
- Insurance wording: "insured up to £1 million".
- No prices, ever. Use "Get a free quote" CTAs.
- Nothing is published without Dean's OK.

- Local pages: 900–1,400 words; service hubs: 1,200–2,000; guides: 1,200–2,000.
- Primary keyword in title, H1, first 100 words, one H2, meta description and slug.
- Title ≤ 60 characters; meta description 140–155 characters including a benefit and "free quote/survey".
- Each area page must include: neighbourhoods/streets, council and parking-suspension rules (verified, with notice periods), building access, nearby areas, FAQ, NAP (Chiswick) or service radius.
- Schema: LocalBusiness/MovingCompany (real NAP), FAQPage, BreadcrumbList. No self-serving review schema.
- Internal links: hub + 2–4 siblings + /contact/ (see internal-links-map.md).
- Check existing pages before writing; update rather than duplicate (Pickfords single-site model).
- Track rankings via Google Search Console once Dean grants access.
